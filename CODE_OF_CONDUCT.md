# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking safety concerns seriously.
- Welcome questions and different levels of robotics experience.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish field or operator data without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, and the publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer using the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
